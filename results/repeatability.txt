## Experiment A2 (estimate, model qwen2.5-7b, link efficiency 60%)
Numbers for Figure 2 (transfer vs prefill):
  - 8k input at 10 Gbit/s: adds 3.24% (ideal layer overlap, 22.37 ms) to 90.7% (no overlap, 626.3 ms) to the measured prefill of 690.7 ms
  - 8k input at 25 Gbit/s: adds 1.3% (ideal layer overlap, 8.95 ms) to 36.3% (no overlap, 250.5 ms) to the measured prefill of 690.7 ms
  - 8k input at 100 Gbit/s: adds 0.32% (ideal layer overlap, 2.24 ms) to 9.1% (no overlap, 62.6 ms) to the measured prefill of 690.7 ms
  - 8k input at 400 Gbit/s: adds 0.08% (ideal layer overlap, 0.56 ms) to 2.3% (no overlap, 15.7 ms) to the measured prefill of 690.7 ms
  - KV cache for 8k input: 469.8 MB; that is 7.34 MB shipped per output token for 64 output tokens
  - with no overlap, transfer takes as long as the measured prefill on links slower than about 9 Gbit/s (nominal); this depends on this GPU's prefill speed
  Reminder: these are estimates from KV size and link speed, not measurements. Do not call either one a bound.
